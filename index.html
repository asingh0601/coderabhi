About

What I actually do

Every app you use has a visible half and an invisible one. I work on the invisible half — and I like the problems nobody else wants to open.

Most of my career has been spent on the backend: the servers, databases and pipelines that sit behind an app and do the real work. Making them fast, making them reliable, and making sure they don't fall over on the busiest day of the year.

I've built for industries where mistakes are expensive — banking and compliance systems, security platforms, logistics. That kind of work teaches you to plan for what happens when things break, not just for when they work.

What I enjoy most is ownership. Hand me a blank page and a vague problem, and I'll come back with something running in production. Right now that's a delivery platform built from scratch on Microsoft Azure — and, after hours, finding out where AI genuinely helps and where it's just a demo.
